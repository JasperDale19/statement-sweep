# Code of conduct

Statement Sweep is a small project about people's money and privacy. Treat
everyone here the way you would want to be treated asking about yours.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient, with newcomers above all. Every site recipe began as
  someone's first pull request.
- Disagree about code, not about people. Say what you would change and why.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you assume.
- Respect privacy. Never ask anyone for a statement, a screenshot of their
  account, a log line with their details, or their sign-in, and never post
  someone else's.

## What is not welcome

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or private messages.
- Discrimination or slurs of any kind.
- Sharing someone's personal or account information without their clear
  permission.
- Spam, or pushing anyone to try something that could put their accounts at
  risk.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment or issue, or reach the
maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept private.
The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests,
and block anyone who does not follow this code.

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and reviews, and
anywhere someone speaks for the project.
